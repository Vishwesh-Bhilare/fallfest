About the event

Qiskit FallFest 2026 brings together students, faculty, researchers, and developers from multiple institutes for a multi-day festival focused on learning and building with Qiskit, IBM's open-source quantum computing framework.

Expect a mix of beginner-friendly sessions and deeper technical talks, hands-on workshops, faculty development activities, and opportunities to build your own quantum computing projects.

What to expect

Four tracks running across the festival.
01

Talks

Invited speakers from IBM Quantum, academia, and industry covering quantum algorithms, emerging technologies, applications, and where the field is headed.

02

Workshops

Hands-on sessions building circuits in Qiskit, from your first gate to developing practical quantum computing applications. Laptops required — setup instructions to be shared before the event.

03

Faculty Development Program (FDP)

A dedicated faculty development program designed to help educators build foundational knowledge of quantum computing and explore ways to integrate Qiskit and quantum technologies into teaching and academic work.

04

Hackathon

Small teams pitch and build a quantum computing project over the festival, with mentors on hand and a demo session to close out the event.
