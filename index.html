A Decade of Quantum on Cloud: Building the Future of Distributed Quantum Computing

Ten years ago, a quantum computer went live on the cloud for the first time — and changed who gets to run one. Join researchers, students, and builders across multiple institutes for three days of talks, hands-on Qiskit workshops, and a hackathon. No prior quantum experience required.

About the Event

Qiskit Fall Fest is a global series of student-run quantum computing events supported by IBM Quantum. This year's edition marks a decade since quantum hardware first became publicly accessible on the cloud — and brings that story to life across multiple host institutes at once.

Expect keynotes from researchers and industry practitioners, beginner-friendly workshops that take you from your first circuit to running on real quantum hardware, and a 48-hour hackathon where teams build and demo original projects with mentors on hand.

What to Expect

Keynotes & Talks

Hear from researchers and industry practitioners on where quantum computing is headed — from error correction to real-world applications running on cloud hardware today.
